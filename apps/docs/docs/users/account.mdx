# Account and settings

Under *Settings* (bottom left, at your account) you find:

| Page | What |
|---|---|
| **Profile** | Display name, photo, university and programme (free text), semester |
| **Appearance** | Light, dark or as the system |
| **Security** | Password, **passkeys** and an **authenticator app** as a second factor |
| **Programme** | Programmes and module catalogues |
| **Calendar** | Timetable subscriptions and your private subscription link |
| **Transcription** | How lectures are transcribed (in the browser or with your own key) |
| **Claude** | Connections and keys for your Claude |

**Delete account**: Under *Profile* you can delete your account. Shared courses stay with the other members. If you are the only admin, make someone else admin first.
