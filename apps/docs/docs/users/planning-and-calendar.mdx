# Planning and calendar

Under *Planning* you see your week: tasks (time blocks), deadlines and the sessions from your timetable.

<Screenshot name="planung" alt="The week planning with the inbox for tasks on the left and the calendar on the right." />

## Views

- **Laptop**: week (7 days) and month. Drag a task from the **inbox** on the left into the calendar to give it a time; select a free area for a new task.
- **Phone**: day, week and month. The day is the start view, the week can be swiped sideways. If you make the window larger, the calendar switches back to the 7-day week.

## Subscribing to your timetable

Under *Settings → Calendar* you add the iCal link of your timetable. Study Manager reads it regularly and turns the recurring dates into **series**. If you assign a series to a course, it supplies the course's **sessions** (with a number, e.g. *session 3 of 13*) and shows cancelled dates.

:::note Which feeds work
The timetable import is tailored to iCal feeds in the style of **CAMPUSonline** (titles with abbreviations like `VO`, `UE`, `VU`, a description with "Entfall" for cancelled dates). Feeds from other systems are often read as well, but matching them to courses is less precise then. Help connect more systems: [Connecting universities](/developers/connecting-universities).
:::

## Subscribing to the calendar on your phone

Your private **subscription link** shows deadlines and planned tasks in your phone's calendar app – read only. You find it under *Settings → Calendar*; anyone who knows the link sees your dates, so treat it like a password and renew it when needed.
