# Documents

Every course has four folders: **Scripts**, **Exercises**, **Past exams** and **Other**. As an editor or owner you upload files (with the button or by drag and drop); readers only see them.

## Viewing

The app shows these directly in the browser: **PDF**, **Word** (`.docx`), images, text and Markdown. Older `.doc` files cannot be shown (save them as `.docx` or PDF). PDFs have pages, text selection and, in [lecture mode](/users/notes-and-lecture-mode), zoom.

## Managing

Through a file's ⋯: **rename**, **move** to another folder or put in the **trash**. You can get files back from the trash for 30 days.

## Finding

The search (`⌘K` / `Ctrl+K`) searches all documents of your courses **by meaning and wording** and jumps to the page. New documents are indexed in the background; that takes a moment.
