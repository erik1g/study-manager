# Courses and roles

In every course, each member has exactly **one role**:

| Role | May |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | everything, including assigning roles, removing members and deleting the course |
| **Editor** | add, change and delete shared things: documents, shared deadlines, shared notes |
| **Reader** | see everything, only create their own things (private tasks, own notes) |

Whoever creates a course is its owner. New members who join through an **invitation link** become **editors** by default; when inviting you can also choose **reader**.

## Inviting members

Under *Course → Members* (behind the ⋯) you create an invitation link or invite someone with an account directly. Invited people see the invitation under *Notifications* and accept it there.

**Other people's courses only appear by invitation.** You see no course you are not a member of.

## What is in a course

- **Overview**: semester progress, dates and tasks, recently changed.
- **Documents** and **notes** (with [lecture mode](/users/notes-and-lecture-mode)).
- **Lectures** and **flashcards**.
- Behind the ⋯: **members**, **activity** (who changed what and when) and the **trash** (deleted documents stay for 30 days).
