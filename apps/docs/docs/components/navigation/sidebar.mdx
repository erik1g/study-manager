## When to use

- At the top the fixed places with an icon, below them the courses with their colour as a dot (`dot`).
- A place is active when you are in it or below it (a course page belongs to "Courses" **and** to its course).
- At the bottom the account and the settings.

## Accessibility

- A `nav` "Hauptnavigation" (main navigation) with lists; the active place is `aria-current="page"`.
- With the router: `linkAs={NavLink}` or `Link` – `href` becomes `to`.
