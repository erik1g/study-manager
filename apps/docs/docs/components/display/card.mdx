# Card

`import { Card } from "@study/ui"`

White with a soft shadow – or in a course colour. Large cards at the top of a page may carry a gradient (`glow`) and grain (`grain`).

```jsx live
<div style={{ display: "grid", gap: 16, gridTemplateColumns: "repeat(auto-fit, minmax(240px, 1fr))" }}>
  <Card tone="blau" glow grain>
    <SectionHeader title="Today" description="3 dates" />
    <div style={{ width: 140, marginLeft: "auto" }}><Illustration kind="schreibtisch" /></div>
  </Card>
  <Card>
    <SectionHeader title="To do" action={<Chip tone="gruen">1 of 4</Chip>} />
  </Card>
</div>
```

## When to use

<DoDont>
  <Do caption="Course colour for things of a course; white for mixed things.">
    <Card tone="blau" padding="sm">Control Engineering</Card>
    <Card padding="sm">To do</Card>
  </Do>
  <Dont caption="Gradient and grain on every card – then nothing is special any more.">
    <Card tone="blau" glow grain padding="sm">A</Card>
    <Card tone="orange" glow grain padding="sm">B</Card>
    <Card tone="gelb" glow grain padding="sm">C</Card>
  </Dont>
</DoDont>

- A card that leads somewhere else: `as={Link}` and `interactive`. Then nothing else in it may be clickable – actions (⋯) sit next to it, not inside.
- No cards in cards in cards. Inside: `variant="sunken"` or a [List](./list) with `variant="raised"`.

## Accessibility

- Choose the right element: `section` with a heading, `article`, `li` in lists.
- Text on course colours always uses `ink` – the card sets this itself.
