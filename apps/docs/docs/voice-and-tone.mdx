# Voice and tone

Study Manager talks like a friendly fellow student: informal, short, concrete, without jargon.

## Principles

- **Short sentences.** One thought per sentence.
- **Say what happens**, not what the system does: "Saved" rather than "The data was successfully persisted".
- **No blame**: "The password does not match" rather than "You entered a wrong password".
- **Proper names stay** (Claude, passkey). Claude is "Claude", not "the AI".

## Buttons and titles

- **Buttons are verbs**: Save, Create, Upload, Delete, Leave. Never "OK", "Yes", "Send" without an object when it is ambiguous.
- **Titles are nouns or questions**: "New course", "Delete course?".
- **Empty states** say "No … yet" – the "yet" gives courage.
- **Confirmations** are short and in the past: "Saved", "Link copied", "Moved to the trash".

## Claude

What Claude did on someone's behalf is marked "via Claude" (a chip in yellow).

## The German interface

The texts in `apps/web/src/i18n/de.ts` follow these rules:

- **Informal "du"**, capitalised only at the start of a sentence: „Leg dein erstes Fach an.“
- **No Denglisch** where there is a good German word: hochladen, Ordner, Fach, Frist. Use the German terms from the [glossary](/developers/glossary).
- Proper umlauts and ß – never "ue" or "ss" as a replacement.
- German quotation marks „so“, ellipsis with … (one character), dash – with spaces.
- Numbers with a unit: „95 MB“, „84 Min.“, „3,2 MB“ (decimal comma).

### Dates and times

| What | Like this |
| --- | --- |
| today, tomorrow, yesterday | „heute“, „morgen, 09:45“ |
| this week | „Do“, „Do, 5. Nov.“ |
| further away | „17. Feb.“, „17. Februar 2027“ in headings |
| time | 24 hours, „09:45“ |
| deadlines | „in 2 Tagen“, „noch 106 Tage“, „heute, 23:59“ |
| ranges | „09:45–11:15“ (en dash without spaces) |

Rules for the English interface follow with its texts.
